# Honda Activa e: — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed as "Honda. Who Else!?" — Honda's electric extension of the Activa nameplate, explicitly tied to Honda's global "Vision 2050" sustainability commitment (Triple Action to Zero: carbon neutrality, clean energy, resource circulation) referenced in the QC1 brochure and shared brand messaging.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as a premium electric alternative to the ICE Activa for urban commuting, with a swappable battery system (rather than QC1's fixed pack) suggesting Honda is exploring a battery-swap ownership model in parallel with home charging for this model.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
PMSM motor (6 kW peak), 1.5 kWh x 2 swappable Li-ion battery modules (IP65), 3 riding modes (Econ, Standard, Sport), 80 km/h top speed, 102 km claimed range (Honda internal testing). RoadSync Duo trim adds a larger 17.78cm (7") TFT running Android Open Source OS with 32GB storage/6GB RAM, Honda RoadSync Duo app, navigation, live tracking, OTA updates, topple alert, and maintenance alert — the base trim has a smaller 12.70cm (5") TFT without these connected features. Both trims include Smart Key (Find/Safe/Start/Unlock). 3-years/50,000km warranty (whichever first) on vehicle, battery, and charger; 3 free services in year one.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
