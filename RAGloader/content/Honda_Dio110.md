# Honda Dio 110 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Dio Wanna Have Fun?" — positioned as Honda's youth-oriented, sportier-styled scooter sibling to the more mainstream Activa, in the same 110/125cc displacement bracket.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at younger, style-conscious urban commuters wanting sportier looks (position lamp, fierce LED styling cues) over the more conservative Activa design language, while sharing much of the same underlying mechanical platform (same 109.51cc engine as Activa 110).

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
TFT Meter on DLX trim (Eco indicator, real-time/average fuel efficiency, range) — no Bluetooth/RoadSync on the 110cc Dio (those features are reserved for the Dio 125 H-Smart trim). ACG silent start, PGM-FI, Universal USB-C charging, CBS with Equalizer.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
