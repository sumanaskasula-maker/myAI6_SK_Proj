# Honda Unicorn — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Be a Wing Rider" and "Celebrating a Grand Legacy of 20 Years" — this brochure is explicitly a 20th-anniversary edition, framing the Unicorn as a long-running, trusted nameplate in Honda's India range ("a symbol of trust and reliability... a true testament to Honda's renowned technological expertise").

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as a comfort-and-stability-focused 160cc commuter/light-tourer — brochure specifically calls out long wheelbase and high (187mm) ground clearance as stability/comfort features for varied road conditions, rather than positioning around outright sportiness.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
160cc OBD2B-compliant eSP-family engine, PGM-FI, Anti-lock Braking System (ABS — exact single/dual-channel configuration not itemised in brochure), Monoshock rear suspension, Maintenance-Free battery, LCD Digital Panel (mileage, speed, fuel consumption, gear position, time, servicing indicator), Engine Stop Switch, premium front cowl with chrome, 3D Honda wing mark on tank, signature dual-chamber-look tail lamp, premium chrome side cover, 15W USB-C port, 3+7 years extended warranty package (10-year coverage).

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
