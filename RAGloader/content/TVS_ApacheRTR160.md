# Apache RTR 160 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS calls the RTR 160 the home of "India's most powerful 160cc engine" (TVS's own claim), positioning it as the entry point into the performance-oriented Apache range.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the RTR 160 as the entry point into its performance Apache range — aimed at riders who want city commuting with a spirited edge, at an accessible price within the Apache line-up.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The RTR 160 offers three switchable ride modes — Urban, Rain, and Sport — each remapping power delivery (TVS publishes distinct power/torque figures for Sport versus Urban/Rain on this model). Its digital instrument cluster includes a lap timer, a track-oriented touch aimed at riders who take the bike to track days. TVS's SmartXonnect connectivity adds Bluetooth, voice assist, navigation, and crash alert. The model also carries Glide Through Technology (GTT), TVS's low-speed clutchless creep feature, and is available with TVS's Dual-Channel ABS on its top variant.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.5/5 rating from 3,347 ratings and 904 reviews — the largest review sample among the Apache models in this workbook. Commonly praised: strong mid-range pickup described by owners as "far more than expected @160cc," smooth and progressive power delivery, competitive pricing and low running cost, real-world fuel efficiency of 40-52 km/l, and sporty, eye-catching looks with LED lighting. Commonly complained about: engine vibration above roughly 70 km/h (reported as numbing to the hands on long rides), an aggressive seating position that some owners feel isn't ideal for casual or long-distance riding, handlebar/steering shake reported by some owners at moderate speed, overheating complaints noted in older BS4-era reviews, and wet-grip concerns raised about the tyres by some owners.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
