# KTM 390 Adventure R — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed as "A New Benchmark in Off-Road Adventure" — rally-tuned power with a high-clearance, off-road-focused chassis. Range tagline: "Get Out There" / "The True Meaning of Adventure." Brochure philosophy statement: "At KTM, Ready to Race isn't just a slogan – it's our DNA. Every motorcycle we build is engineered with precision, agility, and relentless performance that's built to dominate dirt, tarmac and everything in between."

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
The most off-road-capable trim in the 390 Adventure line — spoked wheels and long-travel suspension aimed at riders who genuinely intend to ride off-tarmac, not just want ADV styling.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
MTC, Quickshifter+, 3 Ride Modes (Street/Rain/Off-Road), Cruise Control, Cornering ABS, dedicated Off-Road ABS, Ride-by-Wire, all-LED lighting w/ LED projector, Cornering Traction Control, Bluetooth w/ navigation. 5" TFT with KTM Connect.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
