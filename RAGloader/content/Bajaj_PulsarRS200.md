# Bajaj Pulsar RS200 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the Pulsar RS200 as its only fully-faired sport model, aimed at riders who want race-inspired styling combined with everyday usability.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the RS200's intended use as spirited riding plus confident highway touring. Its differentiators, per Bajaj, are full fairing bodywork, a 6-speed gearbox, dual-channel ABS, an assist-and-slipper clutch, and projector LED headlamps with daytime running lights. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The RS200 pairs a Bluetooth-enabled digital console with a bonded-glass LCD panel and ABS-linked ride modes labeled Road, Rain, and Off-road. Bajaj markets the full fairing, projector LED headlamp with DRLs, and assist-and-slipper clutch as together delivering race-inspired styling without sacrificing everyday rideability.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
This sentiment reflects aggregated opinion across the whole Pulsar (125-400cc range) family, not this specific model/variant, and comes from BikeWale/Team-BHP/MotorBeam/Cartoq-type sources, not Bajaj's own claims.

**Common praise (aggregated):** Engine refinement/exhaust note is often called best-in-class within Bajaj's own range (especially on the N250); strong low-end acceleration and city agility; long-term engine durability reported by very high-mileage owners (100,000+ km cases).

**Common complaints (aggregated):** Recurring electrical/CDI and speedometer faults on older-generation models; carburetion/fuel-injection driveability issues (rough idle, hesitation) tied to fuel quality; noticeable vibration above roughly 90 km/h on newer models (flagged in the N250 long-term test); clutch/gear-shift roughness in stop-go traffic; sporadic brake-related defects persisting through multiple service visits.

**Sources for this sentiment:** eAuto ('15 Common Pulsar Problems'), MotorBeam's Pulsar N250 long-term final report, a Team-BHP Pulsar 135 vibration thread, and a lowest-confidence Quora sentiment thread. (Tier 3/4).

## Competitors
No dedicated three-way comparison test was found for the RS200 in the source material; independent editorial framing (lower-confidence, Tier 3) instead notes it is cross-shopped by buyers trading up from the Yamaha R15/FZ25, and internally against Bajaj's own NS200. These comparisons come from independent editorial framing, not from Bajaj itself.
