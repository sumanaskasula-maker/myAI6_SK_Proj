# TVS Radeon — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS markets the Radeon as its "Commuter Bike of the Year," positioning it as a value-focused daily commuter that emphasises fuel efficiency and low running cost (TVS's own wording, from tvsmotor.com's model page).

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the Radeon as a value-focused daily commuter for city riding, fuel economy, and low running cost. TVS does not position this model for highway or touring use.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The Radeon's headline factory-fitted technology is Synchronized Braking Technology (SBT), TVS's simultaneous front/rear braking system fitted in place of ABS on this model. The range spans a Base Edition with an analogue speedometer up to "Digi Cluster" editions (Digi Drum and Digi Disc) carrying a digital reverse-LCD cluster that shows real-time mileage, a clock, a service-due indicator, and low-fuel warning. TVS does not publish a SmartXonnect connected-app tier for the Radeon; the practical extras it does confirm are a USB charging point and, on some variants, a side-stand-cut-off beeper. The engine uses TVS's Duralife architecture with Eco-Thrust Fuel Injection (EFI), aimed at efficiency rather than outright performance.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.4/5 rating from 641 ratings and 166 reviews. Commonly praised: attractive retro styling, strong real-world mileage in the 60-80 km/l range as reported by owners, a comfortable cushioned seat for shorter rides, solid build quality with no rattles, and a stable, planted feel compared to lighter rivals. Commonly complained about: inconsistent dealer service and parts availability, needing choke on cold mornings, weak drum brakes, stiff/jerky suspension on longer rides, and feeling underpowered two-up on inclines.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
