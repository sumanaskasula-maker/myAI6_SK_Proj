# TVS Sport — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
The Sport has historically carried TVS's "Mera Style Mera TVS Sport" marketing line, positioning it as a stylish, fuel-efficient economy commuter (this tagline is corroborated by BikeWale/TVS marketing coverage rather than captured directly from a current tvsmotor.com page in this research pass).

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the Sport as an entry-level economy commuter aimed at short city trips and fuel efficiency.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The Sport is TVS's most stripped-back commuter in this line-up: a fully analogue instrument cluster (speedometer, odometer, fuel gauge, and low-fuel/hazard/high-beam/malfunction tell-tales) with no digital display, and no confirmed Bluetooth, navigation, or USB charging on base variants. Braking relies on TVS's Synchronized Braking Technology (SBT) rather than ABS. The self-start (ES/ES+) variants add electric start over a bare-bones base spec, but TVS's own price/spec widget for this model was not machine-readable during this research pass, so several details had to be filled from BikeWale instead of tvsmotor.com directly.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.4/5 rating from 1,664 ratings and 423 reviews — the largest review sample among TVS's entry commuters in this workbook. Commonly praised: exceptional fuel efficiency (70-90 km/l reported by owners), attractive styling paired with a light 106 kg kerb weight, a smooth gearbox, comfortable seating and handling, and affordability. Commonly complained about: real-world mileage undershooting TVS's marketing figure, vibration above roughly 65 km/h, battery quality complaints, jerky low-speed running reported by some owners, and an uncomfortable pillion seat.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
