# Honda Hornet 2.0 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Honda's sub-200cc naked streetfighter, brochure-highlighted for having a Golden Upside Down (USD) front fork "first in sub-200cc segment" — a genuine segment-differentiating claim rather than generic marketing copy. A Repsol Edition livery variant (sharing a 2-page brochure with the Dio 125 Repsol Edition) applies Repsol Honda racing-team colours on the same base Hornet 2.0 platform.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as an affordable, sub-200cc entry point into naked-streetfighter styling and handling, aimed at younger riders moving up from commuter motorcycles who want sportier ergonomics, dual-channel ABS, and USD front suspension without stepping up to a 250cc+ price bracket.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
184.40cc PGM-FI engine, Golden USD front fork, Dual Channel ABS, Assist/Slipper Clutch, Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), Dual Petal Disc Brakes, TFT meter (ECO indicator, tachometer, range, side-stand indicator with engine inhibitor), Honda RoadSync, USB-C charging, hazard switch.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No independent, per-model owner-review sample (BikeWale/Team-BHP style) was gathered for this specific model in this project — see Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide notes.
