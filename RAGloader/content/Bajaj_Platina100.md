# Bajaj Platina 100 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the Platina 100 as its most affordable, mileage-focused commuter, aimed at first-time and budget buyers who prioritise running cost above all else.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the Platina 100's intended use as maximum-economy daily commuting. Its differentiator, per Bajaj, is a "Spring-in-Spring" rear suspension system marketed as delivering ride comfort at an entry price point. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Independent sources describe the Platina 100's instrument cluster as most commonly analogue, though one of three Tier-3 sources reviewed states it is digital — a disagreement the source material could not resolve. Feature content is minimal: an LED daytime running light is noted, with no Bluetooth, navigation, or USB charging found. Bajaj's own marketing emphasis for this model is squarely on running-cost economy via the Spring-in-Spring rear suspension design rather than technology features.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
This sentiment reflects aggregated opinion across the whole Platina (100/110) family, not this specific model/variant, and comes from BikeWale/Team-BHP/MotorBeam/Cartoq-type sources, not Bajaj's own claims.

**Common praise (aggregated):** Mileage is the dominant, near-universal praise point (self-reported figures as high as 81-88 kmpl); strong long-term durability claims (one owner cites 150,000 km without breakdown); low running/service costs; good low-speed city maneuverability.

**Common complaints (aggregated):** Weak top-end performance — several owners note the bike struggles past 55-60 km/h and isn't suited to highway use; stability concerns at speed or when larger vehicles pass (a safety-relevant complaint); an underpowered headlight for night riding; brakes cited as a weak point by several reviewers; an occasional mileage-vs-claim gap (one owner reported 56 kmpl against an expected ~85 kmpl).

**Sources for this sentiment:** BikeWale Platina 100 reviews. (Tier 4).

## Competitors
Independent sources (Autocar India, 91Wheels) treat the Hero HF Deluxe and Honda Shine 100 as the standard three-way "first bike" comparison set for the Platina 100. These comparisons come from independent comparison articles, not from Bajaj itself.
