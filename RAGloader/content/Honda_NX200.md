# Honda NX200 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Explore Unfiltered" — Honda's street/ADV-crossover model, sharing its 184.4cc engine platform and core power/torque figures with the Hornet 2.0 (a shared-platform relationship confirmed by matching brochure figures, similar to how KTM's Duke and Adventure families share engines within a displacement class).

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned between a pure naked streetfighter (Hornet 2.0) and a dedicated adventure-tourer, for riders who want ADV-styled ergonomics (upright riding position, tall wind visor, long-travel-look USD fork) and light off-road-capable styling while primarily riding on urban and highway roads.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
184.4cc PGM-FI engine (OBD2B compliant), Upside Down Front Fork (USD), 2 Channel ABS, Assist/Slipper Clutch, Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), piston cooling jet, Diamond Type steel frame, all-LED lighting, long split seats, upswept exhaust, tall wind visor, under-cowl styling, Honda RoadSync (navigation, call/message management, weather updates, music control), USB-C charging, hazard switch, engine stop switch, 3+7 years warranty package (10-year coverage).

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No independent, per-model owner-review sample (BikeWale/Team-BHP style) was gathered for this specific model in this project — see Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide notes. Honda's India two-wheeler business is broadly and commonly associated with strong reliability and resale-value reputation in the Indian market, but that general association is common market knowledge rather than a specific independently-sourced finding from this project's research, and is not a substitute for model-specific verified data.
