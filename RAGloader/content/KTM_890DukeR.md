# KTM 890 Duke R — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
KTM's largest-displacement naked bike sold in India per this brochure set — a step above the single-cylinder 125-400cc Duke range into a 2-cylinder performance flagship. Spec sheet transcribed from a text-native (non-image) PDF, so figures were read directly from source text rather than a rendered page.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
KTM's flagship naked for the Indian market — aimed at experienced riders upgrading from the 390-class Duke range who want a two-cylinder performance step-up, not an entry or commuter-adjacent purchase.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Bosch EMS with Ride-by-Wire, lean-angle-sensitive MTC (3-mode disengageable + optional Track mode), 2× Brembo Stylema 4-piston radial calipers on 320mm discs, Bosch 9.1 MP ABS including Cornering ABS and SuperMoto mode, fully adjustable WP APEX suspension front and rear.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No independent, per-model owner-review sample was gathered for the 890 Duke R specifically in this project. One model-relevant data point did surface in the brand-wide research (see KTM_BrandOverview.md): a camshaft/cam-lobe wear issue reported on pre-2023 790/890-class engines, which KTM is reported to have stated affected a small fraction of units — sourced from a third-party case-study PDF and forum discussion, not verified against KTM's own service bulletins. Beyond that specific note, see KTM_BrandOverview.md for the brand-wide sentiment findings.
