# Apache RTR 200 4V — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS's tagline for the RTR 200 4V is "Lead the pack, Light the way," and TVS references 20 years of Apache racing heritage in positioning this model as a race-bred naked streetfighter with a street-dominance focus.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the RTR 200 4V as a street-dominant naked streetfighter aimed at performance-oriented urban riding, with occasional highway use.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The RTR 200 4V's 5-inch TFT colour display shows race telemetry, turn-by-turn navigation, and ride analytics, and the top USD/TFT variant adds traction control. TVS highlights its Double Cradle Split Synchro Stiff Frame — a patented design TVS says improves stability by 25% based on its own Multi-Body Dynamics Analysis. Glide Through Technology on this model is specified in detail by gear: usable up to 7 km/h in 1st gear, 12 km/h in 2nd, and 17 km/h in 3rd. Styling and finishing touches TVS calls out include gold-finished front forks and adjustable brake/clutch levers. Three ride modes — Sport, Urban, and Rain — are offered.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No single aggregate star rating was captured for the RTR 200 4V in this workbook; sentiment is drawn instead from BikeWale's long-term FI review. Commonly praised: agility and compactness for city/traffic use, an engine the reviewer called "one of the finest iterations" of this powertrain, a low seat height suited to varied rider heights, and sticky Pirelli tyres that inspire braking and cornering confidence — the reviewer felt the bike works equally well as a calm commuter or a spirited performer. Commonly complained about: a sticky gearbox and false neutrals, especially in commuting use, and styling the reviewer described as "outlandish" rather than classically elegant.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
