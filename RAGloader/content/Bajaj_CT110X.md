# Bajaj CT 110X — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the CT 110X as its toughened commuter built for rural and rough-road use, aimed at riders needing durability over rough roads plus light load-carrying capability.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the CT 110X's intended use as rough-road commuting, small load-carrying, and rural/semi-urban riding. Its differentiators, per Bajaj, are a circular belly pan, stronger crash guards, a third footrest, a special carrier, and semi-knobby tyres — all aimed at durability rather than outright performance or styling. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Independent sources describe the CT 110X's instrument cluster as analogue, with a USB charging port as its main connectivity feature (no Bluetooth or navigation found). Bajaj's own marketing emphasis for this model centers on ruggedness hardware — the belly pan, reinforced crash guards, extra footrest, carrier, and semi-knobby tyres — rather than technology or connectivity.

## Competitors
Independent sources (BikeDekho) place the CT 110X against the Hero Splendor Plus, Hero HF Deluxe (including its Flex Fuel variant), and the TVS Sport. These comparisons come from independent comparison articles, not from Bajaj itself.
