# Bajaj Pulsar 180 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the Pulsar 180 as a freshly relaunched classic Pulsar for 2026, aimed at buyers who want more power than the Pulsar 150 without stepping up to the faired 220F.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the Pulsar 180's intended use as daily commuting plus spirited weekend riding, positioned deliberately between the 150 and the 220F within Bajaj's own range. Given the model's very recent relaunch, no external three-way comparison test had yet been published in the source material. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The Pulsar 180 is fitted with a fully digital LCD console with Bluetooth connectivity and connected digital-console features. Because this is a freshly relaunched 2026 model, several deeper technical details (engine architecture, frame type) that are usually independently reported for older Pulsar models had not yet been captured by Tier-3 sources at the time of this workbook's compilation.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
This sentiment reflects aggregated opinion across the whole Pulsar (125-400cc range) family, not this specific model/variant, and comes from BikeWale/Team-BHP/MotorBeam/Cartoq-type sources, not Bajaj's own claims.

**Common praise (aggregated):** Engine refinement/exhaust note is often called best-in-class within Bajaj's own range (especially on the N250); strong low-end acceleration and city agility; long-term engine durability reported by very high-mileage owners (100,000+ km cases).

**Common complaints (aggregated):** Recurring electrical/CDI and speedometer faults on older-generation models; carburetion/fuel-injection driveability issues (rough idle, hesitation) tied to fuel quality; noticeable vibration above roughly 90 km/h on newer models (flagged in the N250 long-term test); clutch/gear-shift roughness in stop-go traffic; sporadic brake-related defects persisting through multiple service visits.

**Sources for this sentiment:** eAuto ('15 Common Pulsar Problems'), MotorBeam's Pulsar N250 long-term final report, a Team-BHP Pulsar 135 vibration thread, and a lowest-confidence Quora sentiment thread. (Tier 3/4).

## Competitors
No external three-way comparison test was found for the Pulsar 180 at the time of writing, reflecting its recent 2026 relaunch; BikeDekho's launch coverage instead frames it primarily within Bajaj's own range, positioned above the 150 and below the 220F. This framing comes from independent launch coverage, not from Bajaj itself.
