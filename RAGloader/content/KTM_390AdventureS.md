# KTM 390 Adventure S — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed as "Your Gateway to Terrains Beyond Roads" — brochure text: "the ideal start to the world of adventure touring... light, capable, and confidence-building." Features rally-inspired bodywork drawing on KTM's Dakar racing heritage.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
The road-biased entry trim of the 390 Adventure line — spoked wheels for light off-road use but tuned suspension/geometry aimed more at touring riders than dedicated off-roaders.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
MTC, Quickshifter+, 3 Ride Modes, Cruise Control, Cornering ABS, Off-Road ABS, Ride-by-Wire, all-LED lighting w/ LED projector, Bluetooth w/ navigation (no Cornering Traction Control — an R-variant-exclusive feature). 5" TFT with KTM Connect (turn-by-turn navigation, calls, music).

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
