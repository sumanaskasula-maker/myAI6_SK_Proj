# KTM 390 Duke — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed as "The Corner Rocket" — brochure text: "the ultimate Corner Rocket." Billed as the most powerful 350cc-class bike in KTM's India lineup (brochure claim; note engine capacity is actually 349.32cc, positioned in the "350cc" marketing bracket). Same brand tagline and stated brand values as the rest of the Duke range: "Naked from the ground up," Purity/Performance/Adventure/Extreme, "Ready to Race."

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
KTM's mainstream middleweight naked — positioned for riders wanting strong performance in a daily-usable streetfighter, one step below the 390 Duke R's outright track focus.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Cruise Control, Cornering ABS, Super Moto ABS, Quickshifter+, Ride-by-Wire, Motorcycle Traction Control (MTC), Ride Modes, Launch Control, adjustable front suspension. 5" TFT display with Bluetooth connectivity.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No independent, per-model owner-review sample (BikeWale/Team-BHP style) was gathered for this specific model in this project — see KTM_BrandOverview.md for the brand-wide, independently researched customer-sentiment findings (the India-specific "Chhapri"/"Khatam" social-stigma pattern and its likely origins, the gap between that social perception and independent professional/expert assessments of KTM's engineering, and general reliability/cost sentiment — shorter service intervals and pricier parts than Japanese rivals, and a dependence on diligent maintenance). Treat that brand-wide document as the sentiment context for this model too, rather than assuming it as model-specific verified fact.
