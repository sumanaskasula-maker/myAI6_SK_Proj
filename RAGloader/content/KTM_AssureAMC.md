# KTM Assure — Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) Program

## What It Is
KTM India's paid service-plan program, separate from standard new-vehicle warranty, covering scheduled maintenance costs. Brochure tagline: "Stress-Free Maintenance — Enjoy benefits up to 30%." Brochure framing: "Your KTM isn't just a motorcycle; it's a promise of adrenaline-fueled adventures. With KTM Assure, we've got your back, ensuring your ride remains as thrilling as the day you first revved it."

## Key Benefits (as stated in brochure)
- Hassle-free maintenance for the motorcycle.
- Inclusive coverage for periodic service components.
- Available at all authorized KTM dealerships.
- Protection against price increases on spare parts due to inflation.
- Policy is transferable to the next motorcycle owner.
- Two program shapes, each in 2-year and 3-year terms: Comprehensive Plan and Periodic Plan.

## Plan Tiers — What's Covered

| Coverage Item | Comprehensive Plan | Periodic Plan |
|---|---|---|
| All scheduled service per owner's manual | Yes | Yes |
| Replacement of spare parts at periodic maintenance | Yes | No |
| Labor cost for periodic maintenance | Yes | Yes |
| Starting price (2-year package) | ₹11,799 | ₹6,699 |

## Terms & Conditions
- Applicable only for KTM motorcycles purchased from authorized dealerships.
- Plans must be purchased within the first 90 days of purchasing the motorcycle.
- Spare-parts price-hike protection covers only parts listed in the service schedule.
- Comprehensive plans cover wear-and-tear components only; accidental damage or misuse is not covered.
- Terms are subject to change per KTM's policy.

## Why This Is Different From TVS/Bajaj Ownership-Cost Fields
The TVS and Bajaj master datasets' "ownership economics" figures came from single-owner anecdotes on BikeWale/Team-BHP — genuine data points, but explicitly caveated as one person's experience, not a rate card. This KTM Assure document is the opposite: an actual official published price list from the OEM. It can be treated as a Tier-1 structured field for KTM, which is a stronger source than what's currently available for the same schema category in TVS/Bajaj.
