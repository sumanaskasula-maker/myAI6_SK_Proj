# Honda Livo — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Live Life Livo Style" — positioned as a more style-conscious 110cc commuter than the CD 110 Dream Deluxe, with a digital LED instrument cluster and muscular tank graphics as headline differentiators over the entry CD 110.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at commuter buyers who want more style and instrumentation (digital display, attractive graphics) than the base CD 110 Dream Deluxe, while staying in the same 110cc affordable-commuter price bracket and sharing the same eSP engine family.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
eSP (Enhanced Smart Power) technology with OBD2B compliant engine, PGM-FI, Silent Start with ACG, piston cooling jet and offset cylinder/rocker roller arm for friction reduction, Digital LED instrument cluster (real-time mileage, average mileage, distance to empty, service due indicator, gear position indicator, eco indicator), 5-Step Adjustable Rear Suspension, DC headlamp, Integrated Engine Start/Stop Switch, Side Stand Engine Cut-Off, 3+7 years extended warranty package (10-year coverage).

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
