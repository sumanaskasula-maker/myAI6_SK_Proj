# Honda Activa Special Edition — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Styled for Stealth" — a livery-focused special edition of the Activa 110 platform, positioned around premium black-chrome styling and two new colour variants (Matte Steel Black Metallic, Pearl Siren Blue) rather than any mechanical change. Brochure framing: "it's not just a scooter; it's an experience."

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at existing-Activa-family buyers who want a more premium/stylised look (parametric pinstripe graphics, black chrome highlights on the front and side-panel logo) without moving up to a different model or displacement.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Same core Honda Smart Key/H-Smart Technology suite as the standard Activa 110 Smart (Smart Safe, Smart Unlock, Smart Start, Smart Find), LED DC headlamp, PGM-FI, 3-Step Adjustable Rear Suspension, alloy wheels, fuel-efficient tubeless tyres, ample under-seat storage, double-lid external fuel fill, bigger front wheel with telescopic suspension, 3+7 years extended warranty package.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
