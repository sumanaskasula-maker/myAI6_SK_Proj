# TVS Star City+ — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS describes the Star City+ as a "Premium motorcycle with 110cc engine, enhanced style and best-in-class features," positioning it as a step-up premium commuter sitting above the Radeon and Sport in TVS's own commuter line-up.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the Star City+ as a premium step-up commuter for city riding plus occasional longer commutes, with styling and features as the key differentiators over its cheaper commuter siblings.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The Star City+'s multi-function instrument cluster includes an econometer (real-time fuel-efficiency readout), a service reminder, and a malfunction indicator, aimed at helping owners track running costs. TVS confirms a USB mobile charging point on the model but does not describe any app-based connectivity for the Star City+ — unlike some newer TVS commuters, it is not built around the SmartXonnect ecosystem. Braking uses TVS's Synchro simultaneous braking system rather than ABS.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.4/5 rating from 790 ratings and 288 reviews. Commonly praised: attractive design and graphics, strong fuel efficiency for the price, affordability within the 100cc segment, and low running/maintenance cost. Commonly complained about: real-world mileage (50-60 km/l) falling short of the marketing claim, reliability/performance complaints emerging after roughly two years of use, engine noise and vibration, weak off-the-line acceleration, and a limited top speed.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
