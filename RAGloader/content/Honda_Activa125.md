# Honda Activa 125 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
The higher-displacement sibling in the same combo brochure as the Activa 110, sharing the "30 Million" legacy/trust positioning of the broader Activa nameplate, but aimed at buyers wanting more power/torque headroom (123.92cc vs 109.51cc) and a disc front brake on the DLX/Smart trims.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned for buyers who want Activa's core commuter-scooter proposition but with more engine performance for two-up riding, highway stints, or a slightly more premium feel — one notch above the Activa 110 in Honda's own scooter hierarchy.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Automatic centrifugal dry-type clutch (distinct from the Activa 110's V-Matic transmission per brochure wording), PGM-FI fuel injection, ACG silent start. Smart trim adds the same Honda Smart Key/Bluetooth/RoadSync suite as the Activa 110 Smart. Front disc brake (190mm) standard on DLX/Smart, unlike the Activa 110's drum-only setup.
