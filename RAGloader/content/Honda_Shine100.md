# Honda Shine 100 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Part of Honda's long-running Shine commuter-motorcycle family, positioned as an even more entry-level/affordable option than the Shine 125 — consistent with Honda's brand-wide strategy of offering a full ladder of commuter displacements (CD 110, Shine 100, Shine 125, SP125) at different price points.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at the most price-sensitive commuter segment, likely overlapping with the CD 110 Dream Deluxe's target buyer but under Honda's Shine brand name rather than the CD/Dream naming.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.
