# Apache RTR 160 4V — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS's tagline for the RTR 160 4V is "Light to lead, Power to win," positioning it as a 4-valve step-up over the RTR 160 2V within the same 160cc displacement class.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the RTR 160 4V as a step up in performance over the RTR 160 2V, while remaining within the same city/performance-commuter use case as its 2V sibling.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
The RTR 160 4V's top "USD with TFT" variant pairs a 5-inch TFT display — showing ride telemetry, turn-by-turn navigation, and ride analytics — with traction control, a feature not offered on the model's other variants. All variants get Glide Through Technology, a hydroformed handlebar, a race-tuned slipper clutch, and a "racing bull-pup" exhaust. Styling touches include a Class-D projector headlamp with a signature LED light blade design. TVS states that a complimentary TVS XPOD helmet is bundled in select states for this model.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.5/5 rating from 2,328 ratings and 748 reviews. Commonly praised: strong power for the class, described by one owner as "a beast in the ₹1 lakh price range"; fuel efficiency of 40-50 km/l in the city and 55-61 km/l on the highway as reported by owners; aggressive, macho styling; smooth, refined transmission with low vibration; and relatively affordable servicing (one owner reported roughly ₹1,200 per service). Commonly complained about: dual-disc variant owners reporting mileage as low as roughly 35 km/l, some owners reporting the front brake/ABS becoming unresponsive after pothole impacts, rear disc brake noise reported by some owners, inconsistent service-centre diagnosis quality, and limited colour choice beyond black according to some buyers.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
