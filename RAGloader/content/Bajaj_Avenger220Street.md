# Bajaj Avenger 220 Street — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the Avenger 220 Street as the street-styled sibling to the Avenger Cruise, sharing the same mechanicals and chassis in a differently styled package.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the Avenger 220 Street's intended use, like the Cruise, as relaxed city and highway cruising, aimed at riders who want the Avenger cruiser's mechanical platform in a more street-styled package. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Independent sources describe the Avenger 220 Street's instrumentation simply as digital, and note features including auto-headlamp-on, a hazard warning indicator, a pillion grab rail, LED turn indicators, a low-battery indicator, and a split-key shutter lock — again with no Bluetooth, navigation, or USB charging found. Note that independent sources disagree on whether this model has ABS at all: BikeWale and BikeDekho report single-channel ABS, while 91Wheels' own listing omits ABS entirely, suggesting a possible non-ABS base variant that could not be resolved from the source material — see the CSV's unresolved_conflicts field for this model.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No customer-sentiment data exists for the Avenger family in the source material — this is stated plainly rather than fabricated.

## Competitors
Independent sources (ZigWheels, 91Wheels) apply the same competitive framing as the Cruise variant: the Royal Enfield Meteor 350 and, at a higher price step, the Honda H'ness CB350. These comparisons come from independent comparison articles, not from Bajaj itself.
