# Apache RTR 310 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS markets the RTR 310 under the lines "Racing DNA Runs" and "The Freestyler," describing it as an "evolution from puritan to play" — its own framing for a stripped-down naked-sport interpretation of performance and self-expression, positioned as the naked-streetfighter sibling of the faired RR 310.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the RTR 310 as its flagship naked streetfighter, aimed at performance and track-adjacent riding. TVS sells an optional "Dynamic Pro Kit" and a Built-to-Order racing-styled trim for this model, and one of its five ride modes is a dedicated Track mode — all signalling a track-capable use case in TVS's own marketing.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
TVS's headline technology claim for the RTR 310 is Race-Tuned Dynamic Stability Control (RTDSC), built on a 6-axis IMU, which TVS describes as a segment/class-first. The optional "Dynamic Pro Kit" adds launch control, cornering ABS, drag torque control, cornering traction control, wheelie control, slope-dependent control, rear-wheel lift-off protection, and cornering cruise control — a substantially more elaborate electronics package than TVS's other naked motorcycles. The bike also gets keyless ride, a Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (bundled with the Dynamic Kit), and Glide Through Technology. Its 5-inch horizontal TFT touch "race computer" supports post-ride analytics, voice assist, What3Words location sharing, race telemetry, telephony functions (call accept/reject, do-not-disturb, SMS), Digi Docs for storing RC/licence documents, a crash-alert feature that notifies emergency contacts within 180 seconds of a detected crash, Bluetooth music playback, and GoPro control. Lighting is an adaptive Bi-LED "Cyborg" headlamp with a sequential 5-LED signature lamp. TVS also states the forged aluminium pistons on this engine are 5% lighter than standard.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
BikeWale aggregates a 4.8/5 rating, but from a small sample of only 66 ratings and 15 reviews, so this figure should be read with caution given the low sample size. Commonly praised: aggressive, muscular styling; responsive power delivery with smooth throttle response; reported highway stability at 120+ km/h; a well-received TFT display and multiple ride modes; and fuel efficiency of 28-38 km/l as reported by owners. Commonly complained about: vibration described as "intense" at mid-to-high RPM by multiple owners, pillion comfort questioned on longer trips, high service cost flagged by some owners, and heat soak reported in slow summer traffic.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
