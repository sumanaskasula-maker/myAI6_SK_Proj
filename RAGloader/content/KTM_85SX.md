# KTM 85 SX — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
A junior motocross competition bike, distinct from KTM's street-legal India road lineup — sold for closed-course racing use. Not a road-registerable motorcycle. Spec sheet transcribed from a text-native (non-image) PDF.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at junior/amateur motocross racers progressing up the competition ladder — sold and serviced through KTM's dealer network for racing use, not for daily transport or road riding of any kind.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Kickstart-only (no electric start, consistent with competition motocross practice), XACT WP Upside-Down fork and PDS monoshock (278mm/305mm travel), wet multi-disc clutch with Formula hydraulics, MEDJ digital ignition.
