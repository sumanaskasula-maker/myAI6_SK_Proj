# Honda Dio 125 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
The higher-displacement Dio sibling, adding a front disc brake ("Sporty Disc Brake — only for Dio 125" per brochure) and, on the H-Smart trim, the full Honda Smart Key/Bluetooth/RoadSync suite. A separate Repsol Edition livery variant (sharing a 2-page brochure with the Hornet 2.0 Repsol Edition) applies Repsol Honda racing-team colours and branding on top of the standard Dio 125 H-Smart mechanical package.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned for buyers wanting the Dio's sportier scooter styling with more engine performance and, on H-Smart, connected-scooter convenience features — the Repsol Edition specifically targets buyers wanting a motorsport-branded, visually distinctive livery on an otherwise mechanically standard Dio 125 H-Smart.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Sporty front disc brake (190mm), fierce LED headlamp, position lamp. H-Smart trim adds: Multi-Function Smart Switch Unit, Smart Key, Smart Find/Unlock/Safe/Start, Bluetooth connectivity, Honda RoadSync app, turn-by-turn navigation with voice assist, calling, message answer-back, weather forecast, music control through TFT, Universal USB-C charging.
