# KTM RC 390 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Brand positioning for the range: "Racetrack DNA for the Street." Brochure states the RC range is "visually inspired by some of the world's most competitive racers" and echoes the styling/ergonomics of KTM's RC16 MotoGP racer. Also sold in a "GP Edition" livery (RC GP 390) with identical mechanical spec, distinguished only by graphics.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
The most track-oriented model in the RC range, aimed at enthusiasts wanting the strongest electronics and performance package for canyon carving and occasional track days.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
MTC (Motorcycle Traction Control), Cornering ABS, Quickshifter+, dual-channel ABS w/ SuperMoto mode, PASC anti-hopping slipper clutch. TFT Multifunction Display with customizable switchgear; 40% larger airbox, curved radiator, clip-on adjustable handlebars with risers, MotoGP-inspired rider-centric layout.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
