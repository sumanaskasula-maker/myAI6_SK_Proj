# TVS Raider 125 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
TVS's tagline for the Raider 125 is "Go wicked with distinct sporty styling, best in class performance," positioning it as a youth-focused sporty commuter built around styling and connected features.

## Use-Case Positioning
TVS positions the Raider 125 as a youth-oriented sporty commuter for city riding, with connected/digital features called out as a key purchase driver.

This reflects TVS's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
TVS calls out Glide Through Technology (GTT) on the Raider 125 as a claimed segment-first — a low-speed clutchless creep feature that lets the bike inch forward in traffic without the rider working the clutch lever. The model offers three selectable ride modes (Boost, Power, Eco) that alter throttle response, and TVS's SmartXonnect suite adds navigation, notifications, low-fuel alerts, and voice assist on connected variants. The top TFT-equipped variant is advertised with "99+ connected features" against "85+" on the LCD-cluster variants. The headlamp includes a "Follow Me Home" function that TVS also claims as segment-first, keeping the light on briefly after the rider parks. TVS specifies its own TRU4 4T 10W30 engine oil (1000 ml capacity) for this model.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No single aggregate star rating was captured for the Raider 125 in this workbook; sentiment instead draws on BikeWale's expert Road Test Review. Commonly praised: distinctive styling with a muscular tank, peppy mid-to-high-rev performance, a light 123 kg kerb weight that makes it agile in traffic, comfortable sporty ergonomics, strong real-world fuel economy (around 57 km/l reported), and full LED lighting with an informative digital cluster. Commonly complained about: a front brake that lacks confidence-inspiring bite, an underwhelming high-beam at night, some cheap-feeling plastic trim (USB cover, switchgear), occasional false neutrals, and vibration at high revs.

The following reflects aggregated owner and reviewer opinion from BikeWale/ZigWheels/Team-BHP, not TVS's own claims or independently verified fact.
