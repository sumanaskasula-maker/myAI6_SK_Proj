# Honda QC1 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed as "Go New. Go Far." — Honda's entry-level electric scooter, explicitly framed around Honda's global "Vision 2050" sustainability commitment ("Driving Zero Boundaries," Triple Action to Zero: carbon neutrality, clean energy, resource circulation, Life Cycle Assessment). Brochure copy: "perfect for today's on-the-go lifestyle, offering smooth, smart, and fun rides. Ideal for eco-conscious users."

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as a simple, no-frills, value-focused electric scooter — brochure explicitly frames it as "free of unnecessary complexity," distinguishing it from the more feature-rich Activa e: (which has connected-app features, swappable batteries, and a larger TFT on its top trim). QC1 uses a fixed battery and home charging rather than swappable-battery infrastructure.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
BLDC in-wheel motor (1.8 kW peak), fixed 1.5 kWh Li-ion battery (BP Cell Chemistry, IP67), 330W off-board home charger with CAN-controlled auto-cut technology (charges from a standard 6A household socket; 0-80% in 4hrs 30min, 0-100% in 6hrs 50min), two ride modes (Econ, Standard), 80km range and 50km/h top speed (Honda internal testing), 26L under-seat boot space, 15W USB-C port in an inner storage pocket, 5" LCD display, side stand inhibitor. 3-years/50,000km warranty (whichever first) separately covering vehicle, charger, and battery; 3 free services in year one.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
