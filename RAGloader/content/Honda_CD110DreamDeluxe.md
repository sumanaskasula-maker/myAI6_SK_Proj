# Honda CD 110 Dream Deluxe — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Marketed under the tagline "Kamaal per litre" ("wonders per litre") — positioned squarely on fuel efficiency as the core value proposition for Honda's most affordable/entry commuter motorcycle nameplate in India.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at highly price- and running-cost-sensitive commuter buyers (first-time motorcycle owners, high-mileage daily commuters) who prioritise low cost of ownership and mileage over features or styling.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
eSP (Enhanced Smart Power) engine technology with ACG silent starter, BS-VI OBD2B compliant engine, PGM-FI, CBS with Equalizer (distributes optimal braking force between front/rear wheels), Integrated Engine Start/Stop Switch, Side Stand Engine Cut-Off, MF (maintenance-free) battery and viscous filter, seal chain (low maintenance), stylish tank graphics, chrome muffler cover, silver alloy wheels, DC headlamp, 3+7 years extended warranty package (10-year coverage).

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
