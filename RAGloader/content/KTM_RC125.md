# KTM RC 125 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Entry-level model in the "All-New KTM RC Range." Shares MotoGP-inspired styling and chassis architecture with the RC 200/RC 390 but has the lightest electronics package of the three (no ABS-linked traction aids beyond basic braking, and a halogen rather than LED headlamp).

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
The most accessible RC, aimed at new/young riders entering the supersport-styling segment for whom this is often a first bike, rather than an upgrade from a naked or commuter.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
SuperMoto ABS not listed as standard per feature matrix (brochure shows RC 125 without a SuperMoto ABS checkmark, unlike RC 200/RC 390). New LCD Dash Display; halogen headlamp with LED pilot lamps (only RC model without a full LED headlamp); 40% larger airbox, curved radiator, clip-on adjustable handlebars with risers, MotoGP-inspired rider-centric layout.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Competitors
Limited direct fully-faired competition at this displacement in the Indian market; the nearest comparators are the Yamaha R15 series (one class up) and naked-to-faired cross-shopping options such as the Bajaj Pulsar NS200.

This competitor set reflects same-class/same-price-bracket positioning in the Indian market; it is not drawn from a specific independent comparison article located during this project's research (unlike the TVS/Bajaj competitor fields, which were sourced from named comparison articles) — treat as reasonable segment inference, not a sourced claim.
