# KTM 65 SX — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
A smaller-displacement junior motocross competition bike than the 85 SX, aimed at younger/entry-level racers. Not a road-registerable motorcycle. Spec sheet transcribed from a text-native (non-image) PDF.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
Aimed at the youngest tier of junior motocross competitors — an entry point into KTM's competition ladder below the 85 SX, sold and serviced for racing use only.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Kickstart-only, XACT WP Upside-Down fork and monoshock (215mm/253.5mm travel), wet multi-disc clutch with Formula hydraulics, HIDRIA digital ignition.
