# KTM 390 Adventure — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
**Important internal conflict, preserved rather than merged**: two different source brochures describe a "KTM 390 Adventure" with materially different power/torque figures, displacement, ground clearance, seat height, kerb weight, and wheel type. One brochure (comparison-table style, alongside the 250 Adventure) lists the lower figures; a separate, apparently newer brochure ("The Next Era in Hardcore Adventure Travel," "All-New Adventure Platform, built with 99% new components") lists the higher figures. This is very likely a 2024-vs-2025 model-year engine update, but neither brochure date-stamps the distinction. **Do not treat either figure as the sole canonical spec — verify current model-year specs directly with KTM India.** Both figure sets, and the full conflict detail, are recorded in KTM_master_dataset.csv (see `price_conflict_note` / `unresolved_conflicts` columns for this row) rather than in this narrative document.

**Corporate note**: KTM is now a Bajaj Auto-owned brand in India (Bajaj Auto completed its acquisition of KTM per late-2025 reporting) — verify exact deal terms/date against Bajaj's own investor filings before treating as final. See KTM_BrandOverview.md for the full corporate-structure note.

## Use-Case Positioning
The standard/base trim of the 390 Adventure line, positioned as general-purpose adventure-touring — brochure language: high-mounted underbelly exhaust for ground clearance/protection, lightweight split trellis frame, "more miles, less stops" fuel tank framing.

This reflects KTM's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
MTC (Cornering MTC per the 2025 brochure), Quickshifter+, 3 Ride Modes, Cruise Control, Cornering ABS, Off-Road ABS, Ride-by-Wire, LED lighting, Bluetooth w/ navigation. 5" TFT with KTM Connect.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty — are in `KTM_master_dataset.csv` / `KTM_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)
