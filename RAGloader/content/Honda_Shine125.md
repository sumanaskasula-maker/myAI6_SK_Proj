# Honda Shine 125 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
The 125cc entry in Honda's Shine commuter family, sharing very close specifications with the SP125 (see the unresolved-conflicts/data-completeness note in the master dataset for this pairing) — likely closely related sibling models within Honda's India commuter line-up rather than materially differentiated products.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as a mainstream 125cc commuter offering a step up in power and features (TFT meter, Honda RoadSync) from the 100cc-class CD 110/Shine 100, without moving into sportier SP-branded styling territory.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
eSP engine technology, PGM-FI, ACG silent start, Idling Stop System, TFT meter, Honda RoadSync, USB-C 15W charging port, sporty split alloy wheels, X-shaped tail lamp, chrome muffler, integrated headlamp beam/passing switch, 5-step adjustable rear suspension, seal chain, side stand engine cutoff, 3+7 years extended warranty package.

(Full structured specifications — engine, dimensions, brakes, suspension, warranty, and trim-level feature gating — are in `Honda_master_dataset.csv` / `Honda_master_dataset.xlsx`, not duplicated here.)

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
No independent, per-model owner-review sample (BikeWale/Team-BHP style) was gathered for this specific model in this project — see Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide notes. Honda's India two-wheeler business is broadly and commonly associated with strong reliability and resale-value reputation in the Indian market, but that general association is common market knowledge rather than a specific independently-sourced finding from this project's research, and is not a substitute for model-specific verified data.

## Competitors
Bajaj Pulsar 125/CT125X, TVS Radeon, Hero Glamour, Honda's own SP125 (internal range overlap).

This competitor set reflects same-class/same-price-bracket positioning in the Indian market; it is not drawn from a specific independent comparison article located during this project's research (unlike the TVS/Bajaj competitor fields, which were sourced from named comparison articles) — treat as reasonable segment inference, not a sourced claim.
