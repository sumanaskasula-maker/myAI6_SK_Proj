# Honda Activa 110 — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Part of Honda's flagship Activa scooter family — the brochure references Activa's "30 Million" cumulative sales milestone (cumulative sales figure of Brand Activa from Jun'01 to Jun'23, per brochure footnote) as a trust/legacy marker. The Activa 110 sits as the mass-market entry point of the Activa range, offered across STD, DLX, and Smart (H-Smart) trims.

**Corporate note**: Honda Motorcycle and Scooter India Pvt. Ltd. (HMSI) is a wholly owned subsidiary of Honda Motor Co., Japan — unlike KTM (Bajaj-owned) or TVS/Bajaj (independent Indian groups), Honda's India two-wheeler business is directly Japanese-parent-owned. See Honda_BrandOverview.md for brand-wide positioning notes.

## Use-Case Positioning
Positioned as an everyday, mass-market commuter scooter for the broadest possible buyer base — the STD trim strips out TFT/connectivity features for price sensitivity, while DLX adds the TFT meter and Smart (H-Smart) adds the full Honda Smart Key/Bluetooth/RoadSync feature set for buyers wanting more convenience tech at a higher price point.

This reflects Honda's own marketing framing and this project's segment analysis, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
eSP-family PGM-FI fuel injection, ACG silent starter (brand-wide Honda 2W technology theme). Smart trim adds: Multi-Function Smart Switch Unit, Smart Key, Smart Find/Unlock/Safe/Start, Bluetooth connectivity, Honda RoadSync app with turn-by-turn navigation, calling, message answer-back, weather forecast, and music control through the TFT. CBS with Equalizer braking (not ABS).
