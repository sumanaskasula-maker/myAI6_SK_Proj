# Bajaj Platina 110 Drum — Knowledge Document

## Brand & Positioning
Bajaj positions the Platina 110 Drum as a step-up Platina offering a 5-speed gearbox, aimed at budget buyers who want a bit more power and more gears than the base Platina 100.

## Use-Case Positioning
Bajaj frames the Platina 110 Drum's intended use as economy daily commuting with slightly more power than the 100. Its differentiators, per Bajaj, are the 5-speed gearbox and a SOS (Spring-Over-Spring) rear suspension design paired with a Nitrox canister. This reflects Bajaj's own marketing framing, not independent test results.

## Notable Technology & Features
Independent sources describe the Platina 110 Drum's instrumentation as an analogue-digital combination (an analogue speedometer paired with digital odometer/trip/fuel/clock readouts), and note an LED daytime running light, hand guards, a tank guard, and a USB charging port. Bajaj's own headline differentiators for this model are mechanical — the added gear and the SOS rear suspension with Nitrox canister — rather than technology-driven.

## Customer Sentiment (Aggregated Opinion — Not Verified Fact)
This sentiment reflects aggregated opinion across the whole Platina (100/110) family, not this specific model/variant, and comes from BikeWale/Team-BHP/MotorBeam/Cartoq-type sources, not Bajaj's own claims.

**Common praise (aggregated):** Mileage is the dominant, near-universal praise point (self-reported figures as high as 81-88 kmpl); strong long-term durability claims (one owner cites 150,000 km without breakdown); low running/service costs; good low-speed city maneuverability.

**Common complaints (aggregated):** Weak top-end performance — several owners note the bike struggles past 55-60 km/h and isn't suited to highway use; stability concerns at speed or when larger vehicles pass (a safety-relevant complaint); an underpowered headlight for night riding; brakes cited as a weak point by several reviewers; an occasional mileage-vs-claim gap (one owner reported 56 kmpl against an expected ~85 kmpl).

**Sources for this sentiment:** BikeWale Platina 100 reviews. (Tier 4).

## Competitors
Independent sources (91Wheels) apply the same competitive framing as the Platina 100: the Hero HF Deluxe and Honda Shine 100. These comparisons come from independent comparison articles, not from Bajaj itself.
